# Model Comparison: Present MESHR Superstructure vs. Liñán et al. (2020)

This document compares the present parametric MESHR superstructure model (Strategy B) against the benchmark catalytic distillation (CD) model of **Liñán et al. (2020)** (*Computers & Chemical Engineering*, 139, 106845, Part II). Both studies investigate the optimal conceptual design of the same laboratory-scale ethyl *tert*-butyl ether (ETBE) catalytic distillation column originally introduced by Miranda et al. (2008) and Bernal et al. (2018), using **equilibrium-stage MESH formulations** with identical non-ideal thermodynamics and LHHW kinetics.

## 2. Explaining the Quantitative Differences

### 2.1 Geometric Consistency on Matching Topologies
When evaluated on Liñán et al.’s exact best discrete configuration ($N_{\text{int}} = 16$, i.e., $N_s = 18$, $N_{FE} = 6$, $N_{FB} = 15$, $\mathcal{R} = \{6, 11, 15\}$), the present Strategy B model reproduces the published optimal column geometry to within **$0.3\%$**:

| Metric ($N_{\text{int}} = 16$, $\mathcal{R} = \{6, 11, 15\}$) | Liñán et al. (2020) Table 3 | Present Model (Strategy B) | Relative Difference |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Column diameter $D_{\text{col}}$ (m) | $0.159$ | $0.1595$ | $+0.31\%$ |
| Stage height $h_t$ ($h_s$) (m) | $0.173$ | $0.1734$ | $+0.23\%$ |
| Aspect ratio $L/D$ | $20.00$ | $20.00$ | $0.00\%$ |
| Bottoms ETBE purity (mol/mol) | $0.83$ | $0.83$ | $0.00\%$ |

This agreement confirms that both models capture the exact same active geometric trade-off between catalyst packing volume (`def_catal_vol`) and mechanical aspect ratio ($L/D \le 20$).

### 2.2 Why Cost Magnitude and Optimal $N_s$ Differ
1. **Absence of the baseline capital constant $C_0$:** In Liñán et al. (2020, Table 3), $C_{\text{inv}}$ starts above $\$10{,}000/\text{yr}$ and increases by only **$\$196/\text{yr}$ ($+1.95\%$)** from $N_{\text{int}} = 8$ (Bernal et al., $\$10{,}034/\text{yr}$) to $N_{\text{int}} = 16$ (3 reactive sections, $\$10{,}230/\text{yr}$). Because the marginal capital penalty of adding 8 extra stages is very small, their economic optimum shifts to a taller column ($N_{\text{int}} = 16$, $RR = 4.374$, $Q_r = 363.2\ \text{kJ/min}$, $\text{Total Cost} = \$22{,}410/\text{yr}$) driven almost entirely by higher ETBE revenue ($\$24{,}044/\text{yr}$ vs. $\$21{,}836/\text{yr}$).
2. **Explicit bare-module CAPEX sensitivity:** In the present model, bare-module Douglas factors ($F_{BM,\text{shell}} = 3.33$, $F_{BM,\text{tray}} = 2.70$, $F_{BM,\text{cond}} = 4.04$, $F_{BM,\text{reb}} = 4.65$) without a fixed $C_0$ offset make total CAPEX much lower in absolute magnitude ($\sim \$1{,}500\text{–}\$2{,}300/\text{yr}$ on competitive designs) yet **more sensitive to stage count and heat duties**. Consequently, adding stages beyond $N_s \approx 12\text{–}14$ incurs a steeper shell/tray capital penalty that outweighs marginal ETBE conversion gains—shifting the interior optimum toward shorter columns (e.g., $N_s = 14$ outperforming $N_s = 18$ in preliminary Strategy B tests) while preserving the key qualitative discovery of Liñán et al. (2020): **alternating, non-consecutive multi-section reactive tray placement** outperforms a single contiguous reactive zone.

## 3. Methodological Rationale for Benchmarking

Liñán et al. (2020) explicitly noted that complete enumeration over the discrete space was infeasible for their pressure-drop MINLP and that global deterministic solvers (BARON, ANTIGONE, SCIP) could not find improved feasible solutions. By preserving the exact **equilibrium MESH + SRK/NRTL + LHHW + continuous $(D_{\text{col}}, h_t, L/D)$ trade-offs** while adopting isobaric operation and smooth continuous-spacing Fair hydraulics, the present model achieves global NLP certification in seconds via BARON. This makes it possible to construct an **exhaustively verified global ground-truth dataset** across thousands of discrete topologies, providing an unambiguous benchmark for assessing the global convergence rate and computational efficiency of decomposition methods (such as D-SDA or bi-level optimization frameworks).
